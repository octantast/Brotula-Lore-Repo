# Genes

A **gene** in Brotula is a permanent upgrade of the explorer. Genes are the global progression of the game: skills last for one dive, genes carry over between dives. There are 35 genes, and each one has levels from 0 to 3.

Related files: [README](../README.md), [battle mechanics](../mechanics/battle.md), [skills](../skills/skills.md), companions (planned).

## How genes are obtained and used

- Companions (mermaids) generate genes. A mermaid chosen for time between fights produces genes of her own type after the next fight, and the player picks among them. See companions (planned).
- Genes found during a dive are collected and injected after the dive, before the next one. The gene screen groups them as new available genes, new inserted genes and degrading genes.
- Up to 4 genes can be inserted before an expedition, or 5 with the Intensive development gene.
- Level 3 is the maximum. Many skills unlock only at level 3 (see [skills](../skills/skills.md)).

## Genes that open cells under high pressure

At depth, pressure closes cells 4 to 9 of the player's grid. Each of these genes keeps one cell open. At level 3 the cell stays open for the whole dive, at level 2 there is a chance to delay its closing, and level 1 has no effect on pressure. Depths are in [battle mechanics](../mechanics/battle.md).

| Gene | Cell | In-game explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Gelatinity, mucus | 4 | amorphousness and secretion increase body maneuverability and equalize pressure |
| Statoliths | 5 | these organelles help maintain balance and steadiness |
| Hard body covering | 6 | scales or shell protect the body from deformation, but impair its streamlining |
| Hydropores | 7 | a special water filtration system equalizes internal and external pressure |
| Gigantism | 8 | the larger the organism, the longer it keeps warm and the slower it functions |
| Lipids | 9 | a fat-filled swim bladder provides buoyancy but reduces the echolocation ability |

Two pairs work against each other. Lipids at levels 2 and 3 only work while Echolocation is lower. Gelatinity and Hard body covering cannot both be at full strength.

## Genes that reveal the enemy's cells

Under pressure the enemy's cells are blackened and invisible. Each of these genes keeps one enemy cell visible. The levels work like the pressure genes above.

| Gene | Enemy cell | In-game explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Chemoreception | 4 | substance concentration changes are more tangible |
| Photoreception | 5 | light and its polarization are more tangible |
| Mechanoreception | 6 | wave oscillation is more tangible |
| Thermoreception | 7 | temperature changes are more tangible |
| Osmoreception | 8 | pressure changes no longer cause perceptual disturbances or destroy proteins |
| Magnetoreception | 9 | the Earth's magnetic field is more tangible |

## Genes that unlock skill families

Each gene below names a family of skills it makes available. The skill list is in [skills](../skills/skills.md).

| Gene | Skill family | Skills opened alone | Skills opened together with another gene |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red pigmentation | healing skills | 10, 11 | 36 (with High immunity) |
| Transparency | defensive skills | 12 | 39 (with Bioluminescence) |
| Black pigmentation | offensive skills | 13, 14 | 40 (with Ink bag) |
| Bioluminescence | blocking skills | 15 | 37 (Pheromones), 38 (Gelatinity), 39 (Transparency) |
| Cirri, needles | ranged skills | 16 | 41 (with Regeneration) |
| Pedicellariae | melee skills | 17, 18 | 43 (with Intensive development) |
| Tentacles, suckers | skills from the middle row | 19 | 42 (with Mimicry) |
| Regeneration | health-threatening skills | 20 | 41 (with Cirri, needles) |
| Teeth | power absorption skills | 21 | 30 (Evisceration), 31 (Toxins) |
| Evisceration | skills used a limited number of times | 22 | 30 (Teeth), 32 (Mechanoreception) |
| Ink bag | mass destruction skills | 23, 24 | 40 (with Black pigmentation) |
| Cuvier tubules | point-focused skills | 25, 26 | 34 (with Colloblasts) |
| Colloblasts | skills for which targets are chosen in groups | 27 | 34 (Cuvier tubules), 35 (High immunity) |
| Toxins | multi-turn skills | 28 | 31 (with Teeth) |
| Fins | dual skills | 29 | 33 (with Gigantism) |

Mechanoreception, Gigantism, Gelatinity, Pheromones, Mimicry, High immunity and Intensive development also take part in two-gene skills, as listed above.

## Genes that change the dive

| Gene | Effect |
|---|---|
| Mimicry | the chance to escape from a fight is doubled (levels 2 and 3 improve it) |
| Echolocation | more creatures are found in biomes: a fat-free swim bladder allows the explorer to make sounds and find objects reflecting them |
| Electroreception | attracting one new companion during an escape is possible, with mucus needed as a conductor of electrical impulses |
| Additional fat pad | more energy to make the first move constantly, and a slightly faster dive (levels 2 and 3 add speed) |
| Additional cavity | companion caviar loot is increased, but the special reservoir is also suitable for parasitic bacteria |
| Pheromones | the player can spend time with more companions: level 2 allows 2, level 3 allows 3 |
| High immunity | protection from defective genes transferred from companions: granulocytes protect the body from poisons, parasites and diseases |
| Intensive development | up to 5 genes can be inserted before the expedition instead of 4 |
| Transparency | also adds a small bonus to the chance to escape at level 2 and above |

## Reduction

Reduction is the name of a degrading state in the gene screen. Its in-game text says that a random gene can be reduced during the next expedition. High immunity protects against defective genes that come from companions.
