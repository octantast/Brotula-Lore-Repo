# World

The world of Brotula is the ocean seen as the memory of water. The game is a deep immersion to the very sources of life and water: not a plain trip to the bottom and not a decay, but a way back toward the origin.

Related files: [README](../README.md), [creatures](../creatures/creatures.md), [companions](../companions/companions.md).

## The explorer

The player controls a sexless deep-sea explorer. The explorer keeps dying and is replaced by a modified one, so memory is erased between dives. After a death the player lands on the capsule screen, sets up genes and starts a new expedition.

## Water memories

Water is one and remembers everything, so it acts as the carrier of the memory that the explorers lose. Water memories drop at random as battle rewards. Each one shows a picture and a text and opens the next scene of a story, in order.

The story is the backstory of the explorer. A man tests Brotula, the first social network built into human consciousness, and watches people around him dissolve into it. Tired of the high-tech world, he leaves, joins the deep-sea expeditions and becomes an explorer like the one the player controls.

## Zones

The zones are part of the lore. Their names appear on the screen as the dive goes deeper (depth in metres, as shown in the game). Different creatures and mermaids appear in each zone (see [creatures](../creatures/creatures.md)).

## The goal

The aim of the game is to reach the lowest secret biome and find ringwoodite crystals, the first structures that formed water on Earth. The biome is left as a mystery: its visuals and music differ from the rest of the game, and its gameplay is slightly different.

## Look

The visual style is monochrome black and white with red. Both the explorer and the creatures can be any colour in that range.
