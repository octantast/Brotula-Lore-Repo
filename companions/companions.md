# Companions

A **companion** in Brotula is a mermaid: a female-shaped creature built on a real deep-sea organism. Companions are one of the rewards after a won battle, and they are the source of the genes that the explorer collects during a dive.

Related files: [README](../README.md), [genes](../genes/genes.md), [battle mechanics](../mechanics/battle.md).

## How companions work

1. After a victory the player may choose a companion as the reward. The player chooses among the companions on the screen. The number of companion slots starts at 2 and grows up to 6: each level-up offers +1 slot as one of two permanent rewards (the other is +1 to the initial caviar stock).
2. Between fights the player opens the companions screen, swipes through them one by one downward and chooses one to spend time with. The player gives her a flower of the deep-sea lily. This is courting that doubles as a study of her anatomy.
3. The player lives through one more battle and returns to the companions screen. The companion that was chosen generates the genes she is able to produce, and the player picks among them.
4. Genes are collected during the dive and injected after it, before the next dive. See [genes](../genes/genes.md).

## What each companion generates

Every companion has her own pool of genes, tied to the organism she is built on. A shrimp-inspired companion can generate a gene of a carapace, a sponge-inspired one generates other genes. A gene marked "x2" or "x3" is more likely to appear than the others in that pool.

Some genes in the pools are defective. Reduction is a degrading gene: its in-game text says that a random gene can be reduced during the next expedition. The High immunity gene protects against defective genes that come from companions.

Related genes that change how companions work:

- **Pheromones**: the player can spend time with more companions (2 at level 2, 3 at level 3).
- **Additional cavity**: companion caviar loot is increased.
- **Electroreception**: attracting one new companion during an escape is possible.
