# Glossary

Short definitions of the terms used across this repository. Each entry points to the file where the term is explained in full.

**Antiblock.** A skill effect that keeps mental clarity while the cell is not extinguished. Icon: an open mollusc with a stone stuck between the valves. See [skills](skills/skills.md).

**Attack.** A skill effect that lowers enemy brain cell activity (-1). Icon: a fish with a mouth. See [skills](skills/skills.md).

**Battle.** A turn-based duel of two sets of brain cells. See [battle mechanics](mechanics/battle.md).

**Block.** A skill effect that clouds the mind while the cell is not extinguished. Icon: a closed mollusc. See [skills](skills/skills.md).

**Brain cell.** One of the nine cells in a 3x3 grid. A cell with a skill is a unit with 1 HP. See [battle mechanics](mechanics/battle.md).

**Brotula.** The name of the game, and also the name of the first social network built into human consciousness in its story. See [world](world/world.md).

**Capsule screen.** The screen where the player lands after the explorer dies. Genes are set up here between dives before a new expedition starts. See [README](README.md).

**Caviar.** The in-game resource and the explorer's food. It gives energy, and energy is spent on training skills. See [README](README.md).

**Closed cell.** A cell blackened by high pressure. It is invisible on the enemy's grid and cannot be occupied on the player's grid. See [battle mechanics](mechanics/battle.md).

**Companion.** A mermaid that the player takes as a reward. A chosen companion generates genes after the next battle. See [companions](companions/companions.md).

**Creature.** An enemy that blocks the explorer's way during a dive. See [creatures](creatures/creatures.md).

**Dive.** One expedition from the surface zone toward the sources of life and water. Skills last for one dive only. See [README](README.md).

**Escape.** An attempt to avoid a fight by tapping your own creature. It is a chance, not a guarantee. See [battle mechanics](mechanics/battle.md).

**Explorer.** The sexless deep-sea creature that the player controls. When it dies, it is replaced by a modified one. See [world](world/world.md).

**Extinguished cell.** A cell that has been damaged. It stays extinguished until the end of the battle and adds 1 to the opponent's total damage. See [battle mechanics](mechanics/battle.md).

**Formation.** The arrangement of the player's skills on the grid, set before a battle in response to the creature's cells. See [battle mechanics](mechanics/battle.md).

**Gene.** A permanent upgrade of the explorer with levels from 0 to 3. Genes carry over between dives and unlock skills. See [genes](genes/genes.md).

**Hint.** The explanation of skill effect icons, opened by tapping the red starfish at the top right of the skills screen. See [skills](skills/skills.md).

**Long-lasting impulse.** A skill effect (effect x3) that belongs to the poison skills. Icon: a stylized sea cucumber. See [skills](skills/skills.md).

**Mermaid.** See Companion.

**Multi-target.** A skill effect marking every skill with more than one target. Icon: neural connections. See [skills](skills/skills.md).

**Pressure.** The depth effect that closes cells 4 to 9. Genes keep specific cells open. See [battle mechanics](mechanics/battle.md).

**Reduction.** A degrading state of genes: a random gene can be reduced during the next expedition. See [genes](genes/genes.md).

**Ringwoodite.** A mineral whose crystals are the first structures that formed water on Earth. Finding them is the goal of the game. See [world](world/world.md).

**Skill.** An action that the player places on a brain cell. It is learned for caviar and lasts for one dive. See [skills](skills/skills.md).

**Step.** One turn of a single cell. A round of a battle has 18 steps, 9 for each side. See [battle mechanics](mechanics/battle.md).

**Trap.** A skill effect: the cell is activated and damaged when excited (-1). Icon: a cave with a lurking creature. See [skills](skills/skills.md).

**Water memory.** A random reward that shows a picture and a text and opens the next scene of the explorer's backstory. See [world](world/world.md).

**Zone.** A depth band of the dive with its own name, creatures and mermaids: Abyssal zone, Hydrothermal vents, Hadal zone, Trench, Trench bottom, Subduction zone. See [world](world/world.md).
